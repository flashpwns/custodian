# Kane Pixels Backrooms world pack

**World ID:** `kane-pixels-backrooms`<br>
**Current pack version:** `0.1.0`<br>
**Maturity:** reference-pack scaffold

This is Custodian’s first canonical-world implementation. Its design target is an
archival, uncertain, physically grounded exploration experience inspired by the
Backrooms works published by Kane Parsons. It treats gaps, conflicting accounts,
and incomplete records as useful simulation material rather than license to assert
new canon.

## Editorial policy

- Record sourced claims as summaries with source references; do not reproduce
  scripts, video, audio, screenshots, or other protected material without clear
  permission.
- Mark deductions as `inferred` and original connective material as
  `interpretive`.
- Preserve uncertainty about chronology, intent, and unexplained phenomena when
  the source does not resolve it.
- Do not present this fan-made pack as official, authorized, or exhaustive.

## Pack layout

Future revisions may add `claims/`, `entities/`, `locations/`, `constraints/`, and
`sources/`. Each record must have a stable identifier, pack version, provenance,
confidence, and an editorial classification. Runtime behavior remains outside this
directory.

## Simulation tone

The pack favors observation over exposition, material details over genre labels,
and plausible institutional records over omniscient narration. A character may
misunderstand what they see; the system should preserve that distinction instead of
correcting it with out-of-world certainty.
