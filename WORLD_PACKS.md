# World-pack showcase

This index may list independently maintained declarative Custodian packs. A
listing is not an endorsement, certification, or support guarantee by Custodian.

## Submission requirements

Provide pack name, brief description, repository URL, Custodian compatibility,
pack version, license, content warnings where applicable, conformance status,
and maintenance status. Packs must not require private kernel imports or
executable pack plugins.
